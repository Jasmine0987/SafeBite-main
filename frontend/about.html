FOOD SAFETY, WITHOUT THE SECOND-GUESSING

WHY
SAFE
BITE?

SafeBite is built around a simple idea: understanding what you eat should not require reading an entire label twice.

THE IDEA

FOOD LABELS
SHOULDN'T FEEL
LIKE HOMEWORK.

Packaged food comes with information everywhere — ingredients, allergens, additives, warnings and names that are not always easy to interpret at a glance.

SafeBite brings those pieces together and turns them into something much easier to act on.

And when a food does not fit, the experience should not simply stop at "no". Your craving still matters.

WHY WE'RE BUILDING IT

THE GOAL ISN'T
MORE INFORMATION.

The goal is to make the information already sitting on the package easier to understand.

SafeBite combines food-label understanding with a personal safety profile, so the same ingredient list can lead to a decision that actually belongs to you.

Then there is the other side of eating: what you actually want.

If something does not fit, SafeBite can move from safety to discovery and look for alternatives based on the craving itself.

WHAT SAFEBITE ACTUALLY DOES

ONE EXPERIENCE.
SEVERAL LAYERS.

SafeBite is not just a camera pointed at a packet. Different pieces of the system work together to move from raw food information to a useful decision.

01 SEE

Capture the product or label you want to understand.

02 READ

Extract the useful information from the food label and ingredient list.

03 CHECK

Compare what was found against the user's personalised safety profile.

04 DECIDE

Turn the analysis into a clear outcome: safe, flagged or unclear.

05 SWAP

When needed, move from restriction to discovery with craving-aware alternatives.

OUR APPROACH

BUILT AROUND
A FEW SIMPLE
IDEAS.

The experience is designed to stay useful even when the underlying system is doing something much more complicated.

01

CLARITY

The output should be easier to understand than the input. A complicated label should not result in an even more complicated answer.

02

PERSONAL

Food safety is not identical for everyone. SafeBite uses the user's profile instead of treating every ingredient list as a universal decision.

03

HONEST

When the system cannot confidently determine something, uncertainty should remain visible rather than being hidden behind an overly certain answer.

04

USEFUL

A flagged food is not necessarily the end of the journey. SafeBite also looks at what the person might want instead.
