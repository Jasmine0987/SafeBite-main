THE TWO JOURNEYS

HOW SAFEBITE
ACTUALLY WORKS

Every scan and every craving runs through its own pipeline. Here's what actually happens behind each one.

JOURNEY ONE

THE SCAN FLOW

01

CAMERA

You point your phone at the ingredient panel — no barcode hunting, no manual search. The camera view locks on the moment it recognizes printed label text in frame.

↓
02

PANEL DETECTION

Before anything gets read, SafeBite finds the panel itself: cropping out the ingredient block from packaging, glare, and clutter so only the text that matters gets passed downstream.

↓
03

OCR

The cropped panel gets converted from pixels to plain text — every ingredient, additive and sweetener extracted in the order it's printed, ready to be checked against what you care about.

↓
04

VERDICT

Your allergen list and flagged additives get matched against the extracted ingredients, and SafeBite lands on a verdict — YES, MAYBE, or NO — with the reasoning spelled out in plain language.

JOURNEY TWO

THE CRAVING FLOW

01

TYPE OR SPEAK

Tell SafeBite what you're craving, typed or spoken, in your own words — "something sweet and fizzy" works just as well as a product name.

↓
02

VAE EMBEDDING

That craving gets embedded into a shared latent space by a variational autoencoder, so "fizzy and sweet" lands near the products that actually satisfy it, not just the ones that share a keyword.

↓
03

RANKED SWAPS

SafeBite ranks nearby products by how well they match the craving and how clean their label is, then surfaces the top swaps — same craving, better ingredients.

THE QUIET THIRD JOURNEY

THE PROACTIVE NUDGE LAYER

This one runs in the background. Instead of waiting for you to scan, SafeBite reads the pattern in what you've already scanned and speaks up before your next bite — no camera required.

01

SCAN HISTORY

Every verdict you've received — clean, flagged, or swapped — gets logged as a sequence, building a picture of your recent eating pattern over time.

↓
02

BILSTM PATTERN READ

A bidirectional LSTM reads that sequence forward and backward, picking up on trends a single scan would never reveal — like three flagged snacks in a row.

↓
03

PROACTIVE NUDGE

Before you even reach for the next item, SafeBite surfaces a heads-up — "before you even scan" — so the pattern gets interrupted, not just recorded.

Reading about it only gets you so far.
