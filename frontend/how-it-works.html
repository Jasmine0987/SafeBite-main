JOURNEY ONE

THE
SCAN FLOW

From a camera frame to a clear YES, MAYBE or NO, every scan passes through four distinct stages.

01

CAMERA

You point your phone at the ingredient panel. No barcode hunting or manual search. SafeBite starts with what your camera sees.

INPUT → CAMERA FRAME
02

PANEL DETECTION

SafeBite identifies the ingredient panel and isolates the useful text from packaging, glare and surrounding visual noise.

VISION → INGREDIENT REGION
03

OCR

The panel becomes plain text. Ingredients, additives and sweeteners are extracted in the order they appear on the label.

IMAGE → TEXT
04

VERDICT

Your allergen profile is matched against the extracted ingredients and SafeBite produces a plain-language verdict.

INGREDIENTS → YES / MAYBE / NO

JOURNEY TWO

THE
CRAVING FLOW

Instead of searching by product name, tell SafeBite what you actually want to eat.

01

TYPE OR SPEAK

Tell SafeBite what you're craving in your own words. Something like "sweet and fizzy" is enough to start the search.

TEXT / VOICE → CRAVING
02

VAE EMBEDDING

The craving is converted into a shared latent representation so similar products can be found beyond simple keyword matching.

CRAVING → LATENT SPACE
03

RANKED SWAPS

Products are ranked according to craving similarity and label safety, giving you alternatives instead of simply telling you what to avoid.

SIMILARITY + SAFETY → SWAPS

THE QUIET THIRD JOURNEY

THE PATTERN.

SafeBite can look across previous scan results rather than treating every product as an isolated event. Patterns such as repeated flagged items can become a proactive signal before the next scan.
