APPY'S RULE

SAFETY SHOULD BE EXPLAINABLE.

A result is more useful when you can understand why it was produced. SafeBite separates learned perception from the final allergen decision, making the safety layer easier to inspect and reason about.

  • Ingredient text is extracted from the detected panel.
  • Extracted terms are cleaned and tokenized before matching.
  • Deterministic allergen rules compare the result against the user's configured profile.
  • Unresolved information remains explicitly unclear instead of being presented as certain.
UNCLEAR

Sometimes the safest answer is: "We don't know yet."

OCR can miss text. Packaging can be damaged. Ingredients can be ambiguous. A system that hides these limitations creates false confidence.

SafeBite therefore treats uncertainty as a first-class outcome. The interface can surface that uncertainty instead of silently turning an incomplete observation into a confident safety claim.
